SunSuno Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

The short version

  • We do not record your calls. Your voice is sent live to the other person and is not stored by us.
  • No name, email or phone number is needed. The app signs you in with a random anonymous ID.
  • Call records are deleted automatically when a call ends, and in any case within about 30 minutes.
  • No ads, no analytics, no selling of data.

1. Who we are

SunSuno ("the app") is a voice-calling app published by RVD Intelligence ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Contact: rvd.intelligence@gmail.com

2. Information the app uses

InformationWhyHow long it is kept
Anonymous user ID — a random identifier created when you first open the app To pair you with another user and to make sure only the two people in a call can join it Until you uninstall the app or clear its data, or ask us to delete it (see section 6)
Call matching records — your anonymous ID, a random call ID, the call status and the time it was created To connect two people and to end the call for both when one hangs up Deleted when the call ends; any leftover record is deleted automatically within about 30 minutes
Your voice (live audio) To let you talk to the person you are matched with Not recorded or stored by us. It is transmitted in real time only for the duration of the call
Technical data — such as IP address, device model, operating system version and network quality Processed automatically by our service providers to deliver the connection and keep it reliable According to each provider's own policy (see section 4)

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or payment details.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone — required to talk during a call. The microphone is only used while you are in a call.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth (optional) — to route call audio to a Bluetooth headset.
  • Notifications (optional) — to show a "call in progress" notification so the call keeps working when the screen is off.
  • Internet and network state — to connect calls.

You can turn optional permissions off in your phone's settings at any time.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions) — anonymous sign-in and call matching. Our Firebase servers are located in Singapore. Firebase privacy information
  • Agora — carries the live voice audio between the two people in a call. Agora privacy policy

Because these providers operate internationally, your information may be processed outside your country.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information only if required by law, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

The person you are matched with can hear what you say during the call. Please do not share personal details you would not want a stranger to know.

6. Your choices and rights

  • End a call at any time with the red button; its record is deleted.
  • Remove your anonymous ID by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. A new random ID is created if you use the app again.
  • Request deletion or access by emailing rvd.intelligence@gmail.com. Because we do not know your name or email, please include the approximate date and time you used the app so we can help.

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU GDPR), you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

7. Safety and reporting

SunSuno connects you with people you do not know. If someone is abusive or makes you feel unsafe, end the call immediately and report it to rvd.intelligence@gmail.com with the date and time of the call.

8. Children

SunSuno is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow children to use the app. If you believe a child has used SunSuno, contact us and we will delete any related information.

9. Security

Connections between the app and our servers use encrypted HTTPS. Access to call data is restricted so that only the two people in a call can read it, and access tokens for voice calls are issued for a single call only and expire automatically. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
